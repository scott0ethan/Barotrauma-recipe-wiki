Tactical Combat Helmet

(sgt_tachelm)

A Combat Helmet designed with integrated thermal optics, allowing for the wearer to have significantly better capabilities in close quarters. However, due to the bulky nature of the optics, armour plating has been reduced and therefore makes it less protective. The lenses are also sensitive to water and thus automatically turn off when submerged.
