Dementonite Spork

(he-spork)

This forbidden piece of cutlery is ever-changing. It will take the shape of either a fork or a spoon based on what mental image the user is projecting in their mind. Unfortunately, such unnatural convenience comes at the cost of one's sanity.

Crafted at:

  • Fabricator
    • Uses 2 Dementonite Cluster
    • Uses 1 Fork
    • Uses 1 Spoon
    • Requires learned recipe from Dementonite Spork
