Fixfoam Tank

(fixfoamtank)

A tank containing a fixfoam solution that can temporarily close off leaks in structures. It will automatically close off new leaks as well for some time. Apply onto structures by using a sprayer and make sure to weld over the fixfoamed area after about 3 minutes!
