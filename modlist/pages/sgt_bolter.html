Prototype Oppressor Battle Pistol

(sgt_bolter)

A short rifle that runs on 12.7x55 by means of magazines. It adapts upon the Coalition's favored Oppressor Battle Rifle, built into a smaller package to increase concealability and without compromising utility. It's known to be unwieldy, often being too powerful for unskilled users, but it fits well in a commanding officers' hand.
