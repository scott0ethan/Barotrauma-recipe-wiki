Can of "Special Alien Meat"

(he-spam)

What ration manufacturers label as "Special Alien Meat" is a blended agregate of poor quality cuts and leftover meat judged not suitable for consumption. The resulting slosh is packed into a can and served only to the most resigned sailors.
