Coalition Precision Marksman Rifle

(sgt_bopmr)

A 7.62x67mm straight-pull rifle, suppressed to the liking of the Black-Ops, using a bullpup frame to allow a longer barrel behind the suppressor, though it needs to be stowed on the back due to it's sheer akwardness. A skilled Rifleman can make better use of this weapon.
