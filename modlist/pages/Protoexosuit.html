Protoexo

(Protoexosuit)

A heavy industrial exoskeleton fitted with a pressure suit, the smaller servos allow for laborius movement when unpowered. Protoexo such as these are the precursor to tho exosuit, and most have long since been recycled due to lack of parts or extreme damage. This rare functional example is a glimpse into the technological feats that were commonplace six decades ago. 6500m Pressure Rating
