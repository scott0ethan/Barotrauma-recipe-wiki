Cargo Scooter

(cargoscooter)

An underwater scooter special-made to transport material underwater. While the scooter is too large to be stored in one's inventory, its inherent buoyancy means the scooter will stay where you leave it.

Crafted at:

  • Fabricator
    • Uses 1 Underwater Scooter
    • Uses 2 Titanium-Aluminum Alloy
