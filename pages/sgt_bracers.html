Durasteel Pacification Gauntlets

(sgt_bracers)

A set of armored bracers that can defend against attacks fairly well and boasts enough weight and heft to hit through armor without injuring the wearer. It has high-voltage contacts interlacing the plates that can send a debilitating current into the target.

Crafted at:

    • Uses 2 Ballistic Fiber
    • Uses 4 Steel Bar
    • Uses 2 Durasteel Bar
