Durasteel Munitions Kit

(scp_durasteelmunitionscase)

A heavy-duty case of Durasteel projectiles and high strength casings designed to withstand significantly higher powder loads. These are very sparingly produced as a result of their manufacturing costs and are exclusively handed out to high ranking Coalition members.
