Blaster Carbine

(sgt_asmd)

A variant of the Blaster with controls more like a conventional rifle, allowing better accuracy and handling, as well as adopting a precharger to make shots nearly immediate, though the recharge may prove to be cumbersome. It uses a silicon gel to absorb the heat coming from the weapon, making it safe to fire from even unskilled users.
