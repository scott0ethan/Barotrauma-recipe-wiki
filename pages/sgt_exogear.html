Powered Exoskeleton

(sgt_exogear)

A heavy-duty exoskeletal rigging used predominantly by miners and cargo technicians for easy transport of materials and heavy equipment, it has cargo mounting points, including one for securing large items to the chassis, and has the ability to handle high amounts of physical duress from handheld equipment. It uses a Radioisotope Thermoelectric generator to provide power, requiring a fuel rod to function.
