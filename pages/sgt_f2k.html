Coalition Suppressed Assault Rifle

(sgt_f2k)

A highly unusual looking suppressed 5.56x45mm rifle featuring an unconventional ejection system and somewhat alien shape, alongside having a full rifle length barrel in a more compact package. Produced for Black Ops who require a covert weapon that uses more available ammunition, though it doesn't accept extended magazines.
