Combat Hardsuit

(scp_combathardsuit)

A powerhouse of a Diving Suit meant for heavy combat, featuring thick anti-ballistic materials and sharp angles to maximize deflection. However this suit severely hampers tool usage in addition to poorer oxygen cycling efficiency. It's servomotors are also incredibly power hungry and rapidly drain battery cells.
