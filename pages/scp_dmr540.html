Raptor

(scp_dmr540)

A highly modernized 7.62x63mm Marksman Rifle hailing from the same failed weapons program as the Hammerhead, being made to replace both the Battle Rifle and the Marksman Rifle. As this rifle is no longer produced, it is extremely rare. A skilled Rifleman can make better use of this weapon.
