Coalition Suppressed Battle Rifle

(sgt_vhs)

Loading 7.62x51, Coalition Black-Ops teams needed something with more "Knockdown Potential," in a more compact package. The bullpup design was used to keep the weapon relatively small and light, alongside integral suppressors to mask their presence when deep in enemy territory. The weapon uses a quick 4-round burst firemode to help with overall accuracy, and reduce ammunition waste.
