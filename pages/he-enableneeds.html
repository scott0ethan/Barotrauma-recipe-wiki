Enable Hungry Europan

(he-enableneeds)

[RMB+E] Enables the hunger and thirst afflictions for the user, causing them to have to eat and drink regularly. This is completely optional and is NOT required to gain buffs from eating or drinking. Warning: Can't be disabled for your current character once it is enabled. DO NOT USE WHEN PLAYING WITH 'NEEDS' BY 'LeDoux'!
