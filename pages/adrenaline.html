Adrenaline

(adrenaline)

A naturally occurring hormone, useful as short-term treatment and prevention for stuns, while also keeping the patient from falling to life-threatening unconsciousness. Can be used to manufacture medicine or combat stimulants.

Used in:

  • Adrenaline Autoinjector
  • Anabolic Steroids
  • Analgesic Reagent
  • Combat Stimulant
  • Fentanyl
  • Mannitol
  • Painkillers
