Blaster Cannon

(sgt_heavyblaster)

An industrious solution to portable laser weaponry, it adopts higher-load capacitors to discharge hotter, though at the cost of a much longer warm-up. Very rarely usable in a firefight, but its ability to burn through personal armor has less competitors than conventional rifles.
