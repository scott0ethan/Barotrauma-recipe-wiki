Renegade Semi-Auto Rifle

(sgt_rifle)

A stark comparison to the more reliable bolt-action Coalition counterpart, it has a deeper internal magazine, with a self-actuating bolt. The gas system is notably unreliable, and pulls enough away from the round to cause decreased performance. A skilled Rifleman can make better use of this weapon.
