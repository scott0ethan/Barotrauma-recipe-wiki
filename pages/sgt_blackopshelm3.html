Coalition Black-Ops Hazardous Environment Helmet

(sgt_blackopshelm3)

An optimised helmet giving full view of the combat sector, suited for Coalition Black-Ops. With hermetical seals and a line for a gas tank, it keeps the user safe from most hazards without complicating the situation, as it will engage the regulator automatically, though it requires the panels to complete a seal for CBRN.
