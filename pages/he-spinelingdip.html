Spineling BBQ Dip

(he-spinelingdip)

Seared Spineling slices with a mound of home-made BBQ sauce for dipping. A deceptively simple meal as many Chefs have left spineling meat unattended on the grill and smelled the consequences.

Crafted at:

      • Min condition 0.5
    • Uses 0.1 Vinegar
    • Uses 0.125 Ketchup
    • Uses 1 Plate
