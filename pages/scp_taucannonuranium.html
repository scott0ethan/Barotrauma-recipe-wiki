Particle Cannon Uranium Cell

(scp_taucannonuranium)

A cell of 10 Uranium-235 pellets for use within the Particle Accelerator Cannon. These pellets provide a small amount of power to the weapon thus making the accelerator eject the high mass particles out at a slower velocity, detonating on impact.
