Heavy Chemthrower

(sgt_chemthrower)

A large, bulky Chemfuel flamethrower designed by the Coalition to incinerate the most hardy of flora and fauna alike, shooting an intense stream of chemical fuel that can reach up to over 900 degrees and be used at significantly better ranges. However it's bulk makes it extremely difficult for a poorly trained user to operate.
