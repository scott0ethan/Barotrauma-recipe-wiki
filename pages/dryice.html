Dry Ice

(dryice)

An extremely dense chunk of dry ice pried from an ice crab shell. Its not pure C02 but an exotic mix of solidified gas and reactive metal salts. If undisturbed it will likely subliminate for months displacing oxygen, can be used as an unconventional explosive but also explodes on impact.

Tags: smallitem, chem, explosive, demolitionsexpert

Deconstructs into:

  • Potassium
  • Carbon
  • Sodium
