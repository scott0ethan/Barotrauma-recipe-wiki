Charybdis Scale

(BTCcharybdisscale)

A large mostly undamaged scale from the Charybdis main body, Slightly flexible it is some kind of organometallic titanium iron alloy that is capable of flexing under extreme stress without breaking, cemented in extruded layers by clusters of oesteoblast-like organs. It should go without saying that researchers and craftsmen will pay a lot for this.
