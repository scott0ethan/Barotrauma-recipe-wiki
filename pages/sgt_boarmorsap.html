Coalition Black-Ops EOD Components

(sgt_boarmorsap)

A set of additive components that attach to the proprietary system of the Black-Ops Rig. The Explosive Diffusal Variants are sensors wired throughout the rig to sense electromagnetic fields and give a view of electrical systems, alongside kevlar wrappings to secure the user against explosives, though these cover some of the color indicators.
