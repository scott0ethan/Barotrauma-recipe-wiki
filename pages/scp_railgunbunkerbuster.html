Sub Buster Shell

(scp_railgunbunkerbuster)

A railgun shell with a very unconventional warhead, with a Physicorium-Durasteel penetrator and staged charges that have incrementally larger explosive payloads. Given how old it looks, it's evident this design was discontinued despite it's incredible sub and outpost-busting capabilities.

Tags: railgunequipment, railgunammo

  • Physicorium Bar
