Coalition Suppressed Combat Shotgun

(sgt_boshotgun)

A tactical mag-fed design, suppressed for use by Black-Ops. Using Renegade-style 12 gauge magazines, it substantially increases overall menueverability of the weapon, as well as reduce the required user-effort to reload.
