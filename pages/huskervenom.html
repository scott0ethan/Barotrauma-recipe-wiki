Husker Venom

(huskervenom)

Extracted venom of a husker, the unique compounds form a complex and poorly understood cocktail of oxycyanide buffered with organic compounds that function similar to stabilozine. Allows the oxycyanide to hyperoxygenate tissue causing a brief aderenaline-like surge, which is toxic to the husk parasite. Valuable and dangerous in the extreme, as the cyanide remains once its oxygen is absorbed.
