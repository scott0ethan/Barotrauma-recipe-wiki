Combat Medic Helmet

(sgt_medhelm)

A Combat Helmet with a built in medical scanner, used to quickly evaluate an injured crew member's status whilst providing a notable degree of protection from small arms. The scanner used however is significantly more power hungry than it's standard issue counterpart and therefore requires third party batteries to remain functional.
