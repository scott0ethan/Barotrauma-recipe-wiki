Coalition Black-Ops Rig

(sgt_combatrig)

An extremely experimental combat suit, designed for the Coalition Black-Ops. A Uniform with advanced alloys and flexible joints, it allows a great deal of protection from gunfire, while keeping the user mobile enough to evade and reposition in the case of overwhelming force. The proprietary attachment system allows specialized components, but they will not work without the rig itself.
