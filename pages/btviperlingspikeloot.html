Viperling Spine

(btviperlingspikeloot)

Rough and seemingly fragile, its just as durable as a normal spineling spike. Thin veins of crystalized morbusine snake through the uneven surface, While it takes a lot of work to poison a target with one of these, the poison can be extracted via deconstructor.
