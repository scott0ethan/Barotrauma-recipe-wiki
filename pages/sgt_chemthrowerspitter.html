Renegade Chemthrower

(sgt_chemthrowerspitter)

A light Chemfuel incendiary device, it ejects a sizable amount of chemical fuel at once, with intent for it to sit and burn, acting as a deterrent. Without aersolizing the fuel pre-ignition, it doesn't get intense enough to burn away flora, instead slowing down targets, as the fuel is very viscous when burning.
