Coalition Black-Ops Specialist Hardcase

(sgt_bobag)

A Hardcase with a built-in range extender for radio equipment that attach to the existing system for the Black-Ops Rig. It comes with a few deeper-than-usual pockets for spare munitions, as well as a sling for putting heavy weapons away, and out of sight. It may need to be recalibrated in a fabricator to use the radio equipment properly.
